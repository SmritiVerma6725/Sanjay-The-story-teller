## 5. Submission & Review Archival Protocol

1. **Submission Method:**  
   Completed worksheets may be returned by the evaluator as a filled Markdown file, text document, or email response to the SignBridge development maintainers.
2. **Review Record Archival:**  
   Upon receipt, the maintainers transcribe the feedback into an immutable JSON audit record adhering to `signbridge_review_schema_v1.0.0` and store it in `tools/isl_motion_extraction/reviews/` with filename convention:  
   `ISL_DailyRoutine_Review_<ReviewerId>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.json`.
3. **Automated Audit Verification:**  
   Archived records are verified by running `python tools/isl_motion_extraction/validate_review_record.py` to confirm schema validity, parameter completeness, and gate adherence.
4. **Privacy & Attributability:**  
   Evaluators may use their real name, official institutional affiliation, or a consistent professional pseudonym/ID code to preserve privacy while maintaining verifiable audit provenance.
