## 1. Project Background & Purpose of Review

SignBridge is an open-source, local-first accessibility project designed to translate captured spoken English audio into Indian Sign Language (ISL) avatar animations directly within the web browser. The system is designed to run offline on local hardware with zero external cloud dependencies.

To improve upon static, hand-keyed animations, we have developed an experimental computer-vision pipeline that extracts 3D human motion from video recordings and retargets the motion onto a 3D humanoid avatar (Adobe Mixamo Y-Bot). 

The specific asset being presented for evaluation is a 3D kinematic reconstruction of the sign **NAMASTE** (Word #0001, ISLRTC Dictionary Volume 1).

## 3. Reviewer Independence, Rights & Neutrality

As an evaluator, your independence and unvarnished judgment are paramount:
- **No Presumed Correctness:** Do not assume the 3D avatar is correct. You are fully empowered to identify errors, describe unnatural motion, or completely reject the candidate.
- **Equal Validity of Negative or Inconclusive Verdicts:** Declaring that a movement is *Unacceptable*, *Requires Tuning*, or *Not Assessable* is just as valuable and respected as an approval.
- **Right to Disagree:** If your dialect, regional variation, or linguistic training differs from the reference performance or other reviewers, your unique perspective will be documented rather than smoothed away.
- **Right to Decline:** Participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to participate, pause at any time, or choose not to complete certain sections.

## 4. Expected Review Commitment & Step-by-Step Workflow

- **Estimated Time:** Approximately 15 to 25 minutes.
- **Available Reference Material:**
  1. **Reference Video:** Frontal video recording of native ISL signing from the official ISLRTC Dictionary Vol 1 (`ISLRTC_Namaste_sample.mp4`).
  2. **Candidate 3D Animation:** Side-by-side interactive 3D humanoid avatar (`preview_mocap.html`) running locally in Google Chrome.
  3. **Cryptographic Checksums:** Provided in the packet to guarantee that feedback is bound to the exact media evaluated.

### Review Steps:
1. **Watch Reference Video:** View the uninterrupted reference video in the left viewport.
2. **Watch 3D Avatar:** Observe the avatar at normal playback speed ($1.0\times$) in the right viewport.
3. **Synchronized Slow-Motion Inspection:** Compare both side-by-side at half-speed ($0.5\times$) or quarter-speed ($0.25\times$).
4. **Step 1 — Record Open-Ended Impressions:** Note your spontaneous, unprompted impressions regarding overall naturalness, posture, stiffness, or cultural appropriateness in your own words.
5. **Step 2 — Score the 5 Phonological Parameters:** Evaluate Handshape (DEZ), Location (TAB), Orientation (ORI), Movement (SIG), and Non-Manual Markers (NMM) using four standardized options:
   - `Acceptable`
   - `Minor Issue`
   - `Unacceptable`
   - `Not Assessable`
6. **Step 3 — Inspect Flagged Anomaly Intervals:** Scrutinize specific frames where tracking confidence was low:
   - **Frame 22 (880 ms):** Initial upward lift stroke from lap rest.
   - **Frame 40 (1600 ms):** Peak prayer hold (*Anjali Mudra*) at sternum height.
   - **Frame 59 (2360 ms):** Hand separation and release.
   - **Frame 62 (2480 ms):** Return descent to lap rest.
7. **Step 4 — Declare Uncertainty / Request Evidence (Optional):** If monocular perspective or camera angle prevents confident judgment, request specific additional evidence (e.g., side-profile camera view or slower capture).
8. **Step 5 — Select Overall Recommendation:** Choose one of four determinations:
   - `Provisional Approval for Further Review` *(Eligible for broader multi-signer panel; NOT approved for production)*.
   - `Requires Revisions / Tuning` *(Linguistic or kinematic adjustments required)*.
   - `Rejected` *(Unacceptable or misleading ISL representation)*.
   - `Not Assessable / Inconclusive` *(Default determination)*.
9. **Export Review Record:** Download your signed evaluation JSON (`ISL_Namaste_Review_*.json`) and share it with the team.

## 7. Known Avatar & Rig Limitations

Before beginning, please note the documented physical boundaries of the current technical prototype:
1. **No Facial Expressions:** The current avatar mesh (Adobe Mixamo Y-Bot) has articulated fingers and limbs, but has a rigid metallic face with **zero facial blendshapes, eye bones, or jaw bones**. A forward head nod is animated on the neck/head bone, but facial morphemes cannot be displayed. Facial expressions should be marked **`Not Assessable on Current Rig`**.
2. **Monocular Tracking Occlusion:** In frontal 2D video, hands visually overlap when pressed together during *Anjali Mudra*. The computer vision model temporarily loses bilateral hand visibility (~49.4% simultaneous detection). The system applies mathematical contact constraints to hold the wrists together; reviewers should examine whether the resulting posture looks natural or artificial.
