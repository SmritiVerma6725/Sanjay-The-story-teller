### Draft 1: For Certified ISL Interpreters & Statutory Bodies (Assigned to TGT-001, TGT-004)

```text
Subject: SignBridge Project: Invitation to evaluate experimental 3D ISL animation candidate (Namaste)

Dear [Evaluator Name / Interpreter Desk],

We are contacting you on behalf of the open-source SignBridge project, a local-first accessibility research tool designed to assist deaf signers by converting spoken audio into 3D Indian Sign Language (ISL) animations.

We have produced an experimental 3D motion reconstruction of the sign NAMASTE (Word #0001, ISLRTC Dictionary Vol 1) using computer-vision landmark tracking. However, mathematical kinematics and algorithm tracking confidence do NOT constitute linguistic accuracy. The animation is experimental and has NOT been validated as linguistically accurate by a qualified Deaf-native ISL signer or certified linguist. We are seeking independent review by certified ISL interpreters to evaluate whether this reconstruction is linguistically acceptable, requires tuning, or must be rejected.

Evaluation Activity & Environment:
- Activity Overview: A side-by-side comparison between the dictionary reference video and a 3D avatar within a local Chrome studio (no video is attached to this email).
- Time Commitment: Approximately 15 to 25 minutes.
- Environment: Local inspection studio (tools/isl_motion_extraction/preview_mocap.html) running locally in Google Chrome. No cloud services, external accounts, or webcam feeds are required.
- Rig Limitations: The 3D avatar (Adobe Mixamo Y-Bot) has articulated fingers and limbs, but possesses a rigid metallic face with no facial bones or blendshapes. Facial expressions cannot be displayed and should be marked "Not Assessable on Current Rig."
- Evaluator Independence: Participation is completely voluntary. You are fully empowered to identify flaws, indicate uncertainty, declare features not assessable, or reject the animation without pressure. Negative feedback is just as valuable as positive feedback.
- Non-Endorsement Notice: Participating in this evaluation does not imply endorsement, approval, or validation of SignBridge by your institution.
- Compensation & Privacy: SignBridge is an open-source research prototype; participation is voluntary and uncompensated. Evaluators may participate under their professional credentials, a pseudonym, or anonymously.

The complete reviewer packet and instructions are documented in Docs/ISL_REVIEWER_INVITATION.md and Docs/ISL_REVIEWER_HANDOFF.md.

If you are open to reviewing this candidate, please reply to confirm your interest, and we will provide the review package.

Sincerely,  
SignBridge Project Development Team
```
