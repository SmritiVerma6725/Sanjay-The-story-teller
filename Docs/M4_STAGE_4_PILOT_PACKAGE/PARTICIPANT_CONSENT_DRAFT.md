## 1. Study Information

* **Study Title:** Usability & Interaction Evaluation of the SignBridge Real-Time Tab Audio Interpreter.
* **Lead Researcher / Facilitator:** [To be authorized by SignBridge Project Owner]
* **Study Duration:** One 45-minute observational session.
* **Location:** Controlled lab workstation or supervised remote session.

---

## 2. Purpose of this Research Study
The purpose of this research is to evaluate how easily users can operate a web browser tool that captures computer audio and displays a 3D animated character on the screen. We want to learn whether the software is easy to control, whether the status messages are clear, and whether the speed of the software is comfortable.

---

## 3. Important Disclaimers & Limitations
Please read and understand the following key points:
1. **Experimental Prototype:** SignBridge is an experimental prototype in development.
2. **Not Certified Sign Language:** The 3D avatar movements in this prototype were created by software engineers. They have **NOT YET been reviewed, corrected, or certified as linguistically accurate** by qualified Deaf-native Indian Sign Language teachers. The avatar may sign inaccurately, incompletely, or not at all.
3. **No High-Stakes Use:** You must never use or rely on this software for emergencies, medical appointments, legal proceedings, police communications, or important financial matters.
4. **Certified Interpreters:** This prototype does NOT replace qualified human sign language interpreters.

---

## 4. Privacy & What Happens to Your Data
* **100% Local:** All audio and speech recognition runs strictly on the local test computer. No audio or text is sent to Google, Amazon, OpenAI, or the internet.
* **No Room Microphone:** The tool captures audio only from the video playing in the browser tab. It does not record room sound, your voice, or the facilitator.
* **Immediate Deletion:** Transcripts are stored in temporary browser memory and are deleted as soon as the session ends.
* **Anonymity:** Your name will never appear in any report. You will be assigned a random ID code (e.g., `P-001`).

---

## 5. Voluntary Participation & Right to Stop
* Participation is completely voluntary.
* You may choose to stop the test at any time, take a break, or skip any question.
* If you decide to stop, you will still receive your full participation compensation.
